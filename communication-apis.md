# Communication APIs

## Twilio

- **Type:** SMS, MMS, WhatsApp, RCS, voice, and verification/OTP workflows
- **Authentication:** Account credentials or API keys; use server-side secrets and follow Twilio's security guidance.
- **Pricing model:** Pay-as-you-go and varies by product, destination, number type, and carrier. US SMS starts at $0.0083 per message in the published rate card, with applicable carrier fees.
- **Documentation:** [Twilio docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs)
- **Pricing:** [US SMS pricing](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us) · [Messaging pricing](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/messaging/pricing)

Rates and carrier fees change; use the official country- and product-specific pricing pages when estimating costs.
