# News APIs

## NewsAPI

- **Type:** Search and retrieve news articles and headlines
- **Authentication:** API key
- **Developer plan:** Free for development and testing only; the provider lists up to 100 requests/day and delayed articles. It is not permitted for production use.
- **Paid plans:** Business is listed at $449/month and Advanced at $1,749/month; production access, article freshness, and quotas depend on the selected subscription.
- **Documentation:** [NewsAPI docs](https://newsapi.org/docs)
- **Plans and pricing:** [NewsAPI pricing](https://newsapi.org/pricing)

Plan names, quotas, and prices can change. Consult the official pricing page before using NewsAPI outside development.
