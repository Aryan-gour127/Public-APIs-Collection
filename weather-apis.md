# Weather APIs

## Open-Meteo

- **Type:** Forecast and historical weather data
- **Authentication:** No API key or signup
- **Access:** Free for non-commercial use, rate-limited to 10,000 calls/day, with no uptime guarantee.
- **Attribution:** Required under the data's CC BY 4.0 licence.
- **API:** [api.open-meteo.com](https://api.open-meteo.com/)
- **Documentation and terms:** [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) · [Pricing and usage](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing) · [Licence](https://open-meteo.com/en/licence)

Commercial use requires a commercial licence/customer plan. Historical, climate, ensemble, and some other datasets may require a higher paid plan.

## WeatherAPI.com

- **Type:** Current conditions, forecasts, and historical weather
- **Authentication:** API key
- **Access:** Free and paid subscription tiers; available quotas and features vary by plan.
- **Documentation:** [API docs](https://www.weatherapi.com/docs/) · [Plans and pricing](https://www.weatherapi.com/pricing.aspx)

Check current plan limits and commercial terms on the official pricing page.
